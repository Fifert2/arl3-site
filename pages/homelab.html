Foundation

Unraid carries all of it

Four 4 TB SAS drives on a flashed IT-mode HBA — a parity-protected XFS array, ~16 TB usable, with a BTRFS NVMe/SSD cache pool and a dedicated 250 GB quarantine SSD. Every service below is a Docker container or a VM on top of it.
